# CODE OF CONDUCT

HALO is a public work for every community. The spaces around it
— issues, pull requests, discussions, node publications — are
held to one standard, the same standard the system itself runs
on: **adoption by example, never by imposition.**

## Expected

- Argue with ideas, in the open, with evidence.
- Label what you know, what you sourced, and what you are
  guessing — and let others do the same without penalty.
- Accept that another community may adopt, adapt, or reject
  your work by its own choice. That right is the design, not
  an insult to your contribution.
- Credit is never required under the license — and giving it
  where due is still good manners.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation of any participant.
- Publishing another person's private or personal data.
- Presenting fabricated data, forged results, or invented
  citations as evidence.
- Using these spaces to impose one place's decisions on
  another place's node.

## Enforcement

Maintainers of a given repository copy may moderate its spaces
under this code; each fork governs its own spaces the same way.
Serious or repeated violations may be removed from the spaces
where they occur. The work itself, once released, cannot be
withdrawn from anyone — that is the license, and it protects
everyone equally, including people removed from a discussion
space.
