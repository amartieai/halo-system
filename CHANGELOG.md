## v1.1 — 2026-11-07

Public-repo polish pass, from an independent public-readiness
review of the live repository, plus the core and the engine
charter (no v1 design content changed):

  and a pull request template.

  labelled synthetic data, with one complete run: question,
  assumptions, steps, output, limitations.

  toward game-grade presentation, with federation, plugin and
  community-extension lanes, and the ungated-platform horizon

  game companies, and platforms.
