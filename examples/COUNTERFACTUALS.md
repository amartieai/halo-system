# WORKED COUNTERFACTUALS — STARTER EXAMPLES

Counterfactuals are the curriculum of the mirror network. Three
founding examples, in the project's own terms. Each is written to
pass the nine-judge gate: stated so it can be false, record
identified, adversary entered, comparison held to the named
change only.

## 1. The Khan counterfactual (rulers and the people's knowledge)

**Baseline:** the historical record as taught — a conqueror
whose decisions, made with his back against the wall and without
any educated knowledge of or from the populace, cascade for
centuries.
**The change:** remove the campaigns — the ruler never does what
he did.
**Run:** the cascade forward through the affected regions:
trade, cities, law, belief, population. Separately, run the
second-order change: the populace of the era gains a working
channel of communication (the modern condition) — decisions can
no longer be made in manufactured ignorance.
**What it tests:** how much of what followed was the man, how
much was the information structure — and whether the "great
conqueror" frame survives the logistics test (what it actually
takes to cross the seas and hold other peoples' lands).

## 2. The burned-books counterfactual (the record itself)

**Baseline:** every conquest in the record begins by burning the
books and beliefs of the conquered nation, so that nobody after
knows how those people really lived; the conquerors then teach
their own ways, and the taught version becomes "history."
**The change:** the books survive.
**Run:** the conquered culture's own categories persist alongside
the conqueror's; knowledge compounds from two roots instead of
one. Track invention, law, and belief formation across the
following centuries.
**What it tests:** the size of the hole the burning made — and
the discipline it imposes on every other run in this project:
the record you were taught is the victors' edition. Declare the
gap; calibrate for it.

## 3. The hydraulics cascade (industry is first-class)

**Baseline:** construction before hydraulics — human and animal
power, slow builds, small machines.
**The change:** hydraulics arrives a generation earlier (or
later).
**Run:** construction speed → the vast builds and gigantic
machinery that follow → what gets built at all → cities, prices,
occupations — forward into the industrial age and the
information age, or backwards from a present-day skyline to find
the hinge invention it stands on.
**What it tests:** that the mirror is not only about people and
laws. A single invention is a cause with a thousand effects, and
a community that can run its industry's cascades can choose its
investments instead of inheriting them.
