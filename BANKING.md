# THE GOVERNING BANK — WHERE THE MONEY SITS

*Written 2026-11-07 at the publisher's direction. The Trust in
DESIGN.md is a fund; a fund must sit somewhere, be created and
distributed somehow, and be governed while it sits. This file
states the design's rules for that. It describes a system to
be built — no such bank exists yet under this charter, and
nothing here is a financial product or an offer of one.*

## The problem, stated plainly

The moment this system holds the world's money, it becomes
the largest prize on earth. It will be attacked — by hackers
after a chunk of it, by states after control of it, by
insiders after both. The publisher says it without blinking:
*all kinds of things are going to happen, and you'll see.*
The bank is therefore designed assuming attack as the normal
condition, the same way the seed assumes suppression. A
governing bank that only works when nobody is trying to break
it is not a bank; it's a vault with a press release.

## The rules

1. **The money belongs to the populace. Custody is not
   ownership.** Every structure in this file is a custodian —
   a pair of hands, never an owner. The legal form is a trust
   because a trust is exactly that distinction written into
   law: what is held for the people can never quietly become
   the holder's.

2. **There is no single vault, so there is no single theft.**
   Funds sit distributed across the nodes — each community's
   share held in its own jurisdiction, in instruments its own
   law recognizes — the way the system itself lives in many
   copies. An attacker who compromises one node reaches one
   node's share, faces that node's law, and finds the rest of
   the world watching the attempt in public. Concentration is
   what makes theft worth attempting; distribution makes it
   small, visible, and unrewarding.

3. **Money moves only through the gate, and only after the
   mirror.** No allocation leaves custody untested: the
   simulation runs first (DESIGN.md), the question passes the
   nine judges, and the movement itself is published — amount,
   source, destination, reason — before or as it happens,
   never after discovery. In the current world, the scandal
   is the movement nobody can see. Here there is nothing else
   to steal *with*: the ledger's openness is the lock.

4. **No person moves money alone.** Every movement above a
   node's smallest band requires multiple independent
   authorizations, from people and systems that do not report
   to each other — and the AI administration (DESIGN.md) is
   one voice among them, never the only one. The historical
   disease is a signature nobody questioned; this system has
   no unquestioned signatures anywhere in it.

5. **Creation is law, not discretion.** How new funds come
   into existence — the doctrine's sovereign mechanism
   (Canadian People's Trust / Longo Doctrine papers) — is
   fixed in the open charter, identical everywhere, and
   changeable only the way the seed changes: through the
   gate, in public, versioned. A money-creation power that
   can be quietly adjusted is a printing press with an owner;
   this design has no owner.

6. **Assume breach; rehearse it.** Nodes run attack
   simulations against their own custody the way they run
   every other scenario — the red team is part of the
   calendar, not a response to embarrassment. A breach, when
   it comes, is published with the same completeness as an
   allocation: what was reached, what was taken, how it got
   in, what changed. The system's answer to "you'll see" is:
   *yes — and so will everyone, immediately, in full.*

7. **Local rails first, exotic rails by choice.** Payments
   run on each place's ordinary, boring, well-defended
   banking infrastructure first. Crypto rails are a node's
   option where its community chooses them (see
   CORE-SEED.md), never a requirement imposed by the centre —
   the centre imposes nothing but the floor.

## What this file does not do

It does not name a custodian, open an account, set an amount,
or move a cent. Those are acts for real communities standing
up real nodes under real law, graded at the gate like
everything else. What it does is fix the *shape* money must
take inside this system before the first dollar exists — so
that the can of worms the publisher named is opened here, on
paper, in daylight, by design — instead of later, in panic,
by whoever gets there first.
