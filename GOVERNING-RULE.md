# THE GOVERNING RULE — THE ONE SHARED FLOOR

Federation is not uniformity. Under HALO, cities, provinces, and
nations keep their own categories, beliefs, calibrations, and
laws. Exactly **one** layer is shared by every participating
community:

> **One governing rule of law that all abide by** — minimal,
> universal, and advertised.

The founding analogy is the Ten Commandments: not a legal code
for daily life, but a short floor of prohibitions and duties that
sits underneath every local variation and is the same in every
place that joins.

## Properties the rule must have

- **Short.** If it cannot be stated plainly, it is a code, not a
  floor — and codes are where hiding starts.
- **Universal.** It binds the governors exactly as it binds the
  governed. The historical disease this whole system answers is
  a class placed above the law *by hiding*; the floor exists so
  that no one is above it in the open.
- **Advertised.** Published everywhere the system runs, including
  what it means for the social-media stance: the shared channel
  is ungated, programming-by-fear is a named violation of the
  floor's spirit, and any community may inspect any other's
  published results.
- **Adopted, not imposed.** A community joins by its own people's
  decision, having seen the rule and the results of those already
  under it. A community that declines is a neighbour, not an
  enemy.

## The slot is open

This file defines the slot — its size, weight, and position in
the architecture. The rule's final text is for the participating
communities and the world's reasoners to settle in the open,
through the same nine-judge gate as every other question in the
system. Drafts are welcome as issues and pull requests, graded
like everything else here.
